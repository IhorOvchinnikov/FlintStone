How We Onboard Brand Partners

Transparent three-step process designed for seamless vendor integration.

Step 01

Submit Catalog & Inquiry

Share your wholesale catalog or product sheet. Our purchasing team evaluates potential volume and marketplace strategy within 24 hours.

Step 02

Review & Agreement

We review and sign your brand policies, Resale Agreements, and MAP guidelines to ensure full pricing alignment.

Step 03

PO Execution & Logistics

Initial Purchase Orders (PO) are issued with fast payment terms and direct shipment to our certified U.S. 3PL prep network.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries from prospective brand owners and authorized distributors.

Do you sign Authorized Dealer & MAP Agreements?

Yes. We strictly adhere to manufacturer MAP policies and execute formal dealer agreements prior to placing commercial orders.

What payment terms do you support?

We support immediate ACH Wire / Credit Card payments for initial opening orders, as well as Net 30 terms for established partnerships.

Where are goods shipped and processed?

All inbound shipments are routed through our vetted U.S. 3PL prep facilities for strict quality inspection and FBA barcoding.
