Amazon FBA Logistics & Distribution

End-to-end e-commerce supply chain management. We purchase inventory wholesale, handle compliant 3PL preparation, and manage Prime fulfillment routing.

Professional FBA Prep

Every shipment is processed through professional U.S. prep facilities. We handle individual product inspection, bubble wrapping, and polybagging according to strict Amazon guidelines.

FNSKU Barcoding

We apply individual FNSKU barcode labels over existing UPC codes, helping ensure correct product identification, inventory isolation, and supply chain traceability.

Bundling & Multi-Packs

We create compliant multi-pack configurations and custom product bundles to optimize shipping costs and increase average order values for your brand catalog.

Palletization & Freight Routing

Our logistics team coordinates master carton packing, LTL/FTL palletization, and Amazon-compliant freight dispatch directly to assigned fulfillment centers.

Inventory Replenishment

We use inventory planning and demand forecasting tools to support healthy stock levels, avoiding stockouts while preventing long-term storage fee penalties.

Marketplace & Catalog Support

We monitor catalog status, listing compliance, and marketplace availability to support a healthy brand presence on Amazon.

The FBA Logistics Lifecycle

How we transform wholesale inventory into marketplace-ready Amazon Prime listings.

STAGE 01

Inbound Freight Reception

Shipments arrive directly from manufacturers at our U.S.-based 3PL warehousing and prep network.

STAGE 02

Inspection & Labeling

Physical quality control check, packaging compliance, and FNSKU barcoding per SKU.

STAGE 03

Fulfillment Center Routing

Creation of Amazon shipment plans, pallet prep, and freight dispatch to assigned fulfillment centers.

STAGE 04

Prime Inventory Active

Products become available through Amazon FBA and eligible Prime fulfillment, subject to Amazon’s fulfillment and eligibility requirements.
