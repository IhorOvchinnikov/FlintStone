    🛡️ Our Statement on Minimum Advertised Price (MAP)

    At Flint & Stone Commerce LLC, we believe that sustainable retail growth is built on trust and price integrity. We understand that unrestricted price erosion and unauthorized discounting ruin brand equity and harm relationship with brick-and-mortar as well as online distributors.

    We officially commit to strictly maintaining the Minimum Advertised Price (MAP) policies established by all our manufacturing and distribution partners across every sales channel we operate.

    ⚙️ How We Enforce MAP Compliance

    We do not rely on manual price adjustments or speculative repricing strategies. Our sales infrastructure incorporates systematic price controls designed to prevent accidental violations:

    Automated Price Floors

    Our algorithmic repricing software is pre-configured with strict minimum price limits tied directly to supplier MAP agreements. Pricing cannot drop below authorized thresholds under any circumstances.

    Continuous Monitoring

    We monitor listing pricing 24/7. If external market suppression occurs, our system automatically locks prices at the MAP level rather than participating in destructive price wars.

    Promotional Alignment

    We coordinate all temporary sales, coupons, and promotional discounts directly with brand management, ensuring full compliance prior to campaign launches.

    🤝 Active Brand Collaboration

    Beyond our own compliance, we actively assist our brand partners in identifying rogue sellers, counterfeit listings, and unauthorized distribution leaks on digital marketplaces.

    When you partner with Flint & Stone Commerce LLC, you gain a vigilant ally dedicated to maintaining a clean, predictable, and profitable marketplace ecosystem for your products.
