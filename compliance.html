MAP Compliance & Brand Protection

Your brand equity is our priority. We enforce zero-tolerance price protection policies to maintain retail channel integrity across online marketplaces.

Hard Price Floors

We lock Minimum Advertised Price (MAP) thresholds directly into our inventory repricing software. Your listing price will never drop below your specified policy floor.

No Race-to-the-Bottom Repricing

Uncontrolled automated repricing destroys brand perception. We do not participate in destructive price wars that erode retail margin or brand prestige.

Authorized Reseller Agreements

We sign and strictly follow your official Reseller Agreements, Authorized Dealer Policies, and non-liquidation terms prior to receiving inventory.

Marketplace Leak Identification

We assist brand partners by flagging unauthorized liquidators and grey-market sellers who undercut official pricing on marketplace listings.

How We Enforce Price Compliance

Our 4-step framework for maintaining marketplace channel stability.

PHASE 1

Policy Onboarding

We document your official MAP guidelines, minimum prices, and promotional calendars in our central system.

PHASE 2

System Integration

Price floors are programmed into our catalog management tools, blocking any sale below your threshold.

PHASE 3

Continuous Audit

We continuously monitor buy-box dynamics and notify brand managers of rogue unauthorized sellers.

PHASE 4

Long-Term Stability

By enforcing pricing, we preserve healthy retail margins for your brand across all distribution channels.
