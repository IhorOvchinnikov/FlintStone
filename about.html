About IO Wholesale Group

Your value-added partner in authorized U.S. wholesale distribution and digital growth.

Empowering Brands in Modern Retail

IO Wholesale Group LLC is a legally registered U.S. wholesale distributor and retail growth partner based in Sheridan, Wyoming. We act as a critical bridge between premium product manufacturers and rapidly growing digital market channels.

Unlike traditional distributors who simply move boxes, we function as brand guardians. Our team combines supply chain efficiency with advanced pricing compliance, ensuring that your products achieve high sales velocity without risking brand integrity or price stability.

We work exclusively with authorized manufacturers, verified B2B vendors, and brand owners who demand operational transparency, reliable purchase orders, and complete channel control.

Direct B2B Supply Chain

We order inventory directly from brand owners and authorized master distributors, streamlining logistics and eliminating gray-market risks.

Capital & Cash-Flow Reliability

We place consistent, recurring wholesale purchase orders that give brand manufacturers predictable cash flow and dependable inventory forecasting.

End-to-End Fulfillment Integration

Equipped with specialized prep center capabilities, we handle custom bundling, FN-SKU labeling, palletization, and direct-to-FBA logistics.
