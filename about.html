About Flint & Stone Commerce

Building long-term, value-driven wholesale partnerships across North American digital sales channels.

Your Trusted U.S. Distribution Partner

Flint & Stone Commerce LLC is a legally registered U.S. wholesale enterprise headquartered in Sheridan, Wyoming. We specialize in sourcing high-quality consumer goods directly from manufacturers, brand owners, and primary authorized distributors.

Unlike conventional online retailers who engage in speculative repricing, we operate as a full-service B2B distribution partner. We leverage market data and streamlined prep infrastructure to accelerate brand presence without compromising retail price integrity.

Our Mission & Strategic Focus

Our core mission is straightforward: enable brand manufacturers to scale their sales volume seamlessly across top digital marketplaces while completely eliminating price erosion.

We honor every brand partner's guidelines, enforce strict Minimum Advertised Price (MAP) compliance, and deliver consistent, recurring purchase orders that strengthen supply chain predictability.

Our Operational Pillars

How we maintain standard-setting performance for our brand partners.

Brand Equity Protection

We respect your MAP policies and dealer guidelines. We never compromise long-term brand equity for short-term sales velocity.

Certified Prep & Inspection

Every shipment is processed through compliant U.S. prep centers for inspection, barcoding, and marketplace-ready freight forwarding.

Data-Driven Demand Planning

We employ market analytics software to project inventory turnover rates, ensuring prompt re-orders before stockouts occur.

Responsive B2B Communication

We maintain clear lines of communication with vendor accounts. Our procurement team is accessible for order coordination and updates.

Ready to Authorize a New Wholesale Partner?

We are fully equipped with State Resale Certificates, U.S. Tax ID (EIN), and standard NDA agreements for rapid vendor onboarding.
