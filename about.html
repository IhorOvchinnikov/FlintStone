About IO Wholesale Group

A U.S. wholesale enterprise dedicated to transparent B2B purchasing, traceable supply chains, and Amazon marketplace growth.

Your U.S. Wholesale Partner

IO Wholesale Group LLC is a legally registered U.S. wholesale enterprise headquartered in Sheridan, Wyoming. We specialize in sourcing high-quality consumer goods directly from brand owners and primary authorized distributors.

We operate as a value-added B2B buyer. Instead of speculative online retailing, we purchase inventory via formal Purchase Orders and manage compliant Amazon FBA distribution while preserving brand pricing integrity.

Our Mission & Strategic Focus

Our core mission is to enable brand manufacturers to scale sales volume across top digital sales channels while completely eliminating price erosion and unauthorized resellers.

We strictly honor dealer agreements, enforce Minimum Advertised Price (MAP) guidelines, and deliver consistent, recurring purchase orders that strengthen supply chain predictability.
