About IO Wholesale Group

An independent wholesale distribution partner focused on brand integrity, MAP compliance, and controlled marketplace growth.

Our Retail Approach

IO Wholesale Group is a privately held retail partner for brand manufacturers and authorized distributors. We specialize in expanding product reach across Amazon and secondary e-commerce channels without compromising brand positioning.

We are not third-party speculators or drop-shippers. We allocate our own capital to purchase inventory upfront via standard Purchase Orders (POs). Once we take ownership, we manage the end-to-end logistical complexity of Amazon FBA.

Supply Chain Integrity

Sustainable marketplace distribution requires absolute transparency. Every unit we purchase is sourced strictly through authorized channels, ensuring full invoice traceability and eliminating the risk of parallel or gray-market imports.

Through our dedicated 3PL warehouse infrastructure, we enforce rigorous quality control, accurate FNSKU barcoding, and disciplined adherence to Amazon’s inbound routing guidelines.

Upfront Purchasing

Direct B2B purchase orders with transparent payment terms and predictable, data-driven replenishment cycles.

Strict MAP Adherence

Zero-tolerance policy for price erosion. We safeguard your offline retail margins by holding the line on digital channels.

FBA-Ready Logistics

Professional inspection, poly-bagging, and compliant palletization executed seamlessly before hitting Amazon.
