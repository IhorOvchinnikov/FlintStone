About Flint & Stone Commerce

Building long-term, value-driven wholesale partnerships across digital sales channels.

Your Trusted U.S. Distribution Partner

Flint & Stone Commerce LLC is a registered U.S. wholesale enterprise based in Sheridan, Wyoming. We specialize in sourcing quality consumer goods directly from manufacturers, brand owners, and authorized distributors.

Unlike traditional retailers, we operate as a full-service brand partner. We utilize advanced market analytics and streamlined logistics to expand brand availability while actively protecting market integrity.

Our Mission & Strategic Goal

Our goal is simple: to help brand owners scale their sales volume seamlessly without compromising their brand positioning or pricing standards.

We treat every brand partner's catalog with respect, strictly adhering to Minimum Advertised Price (MAP) guidelines and providing steady, recurring wholesale orders.

Core Partnership Principles

🤝 Brand Integrity First

We respect your pricing policies and brand guidelines. We never compromise long-term brand health for short-term gains.

📦 Certified Preparation

All products are processed through certified prep facilities, ensuring strict compliance with marketplace shipping standards.

📊 Data-Driven Purchasing

Our sophisticated inventory management systems ensure predictable demand forecasting and consistent replenishment orders.

⚡ Fast Communication

We believe in transparent, reliable B2B communication. Our purchasing team is always accessible for our supply chain partners.
